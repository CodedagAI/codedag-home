# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

If you find a security vulnerability in CodeDAG, including the CodeDAG CLI, codedag.com and app.codedag.com, report it to us privately. Don't open a public issue.

Email hi@codedag.com with a subject line that starts with `Security:`, and include:

- What the vulnerability is and what an attacker could do with it.
- Steps to reproduce it, or a proof of concept.
- The affected component and version, such as the output of `cdg --version` or the address of the affected page.

Don't include other people's data or real secrets in your report. Use test values instead.

We'll reply to confirm that we received your report and keep you updated while we work on a fix. Please don't disclose the vulnerability publicly until we've released a fix or agreed on a disclosure date with you.

## Testing guidelines

When you look for vulnerabilities, use only accounts you own, don't access or change other users' data, and don't run tests that degrade the service for others, such as denial-of-service attacks.

We don't run a bug bounty program at this time.
