This policy explains when Zillion AI LLC ("Zillion AI", "we", "us") refunds payments for CodeDAG subscriptions. It's part of our [Terms of Service](/terms), which explain how subscriptions renew and how to [cancel](/terms#cancellation).

## 14-day refund [#14-day-refund]

If you ask within 14 days of either of these payments, you can get a full refund without giving a reason:

- the first payment of your first CodeDAG subscription;
- any yearly payment, including when you start a yearly subscription, switch from monthly to yearly billing, or your yearly subscription renews.

When we make this refund, your subscription ends right away. If you start a new subscription after receiving a 14-day refund, its first payment isn't covered, even if it's a yearly payment.

## Other refunds [#when-we-refund]

We also refund a payment, in whole or in part, in these cases:

- **Charges made in error:** for example, a duplicate charge, a charge of the wrong amount, or a renewal charged after your cancellation took effect.
- **When we don't provide what you paid for:** if a problem on our side keeps the Service from working for a significant time, if we close your account for a reason other than your breach of the Terms, or if we discontinue the Service, we refund the affected or unused part of your billing period.
- **When you end your subscription because of a change:** if a change to [the Service](/terms#service-changes) or to [the Terms](/terms#changes) lets you end your subscription, write to us within the period the Terms set. We end your subscription when we receive your request and refund the unused part of your current billing period.
- **When the law gives you a right to a refund:** for example, a right to withdraw from a purchase, to end a yearly subscription early, or to cancel after a price increase or a renewal. This policy doesn't limit those rights.

## Payments we don't refund [#cancel]

Outside the cases above, payments aren't refunded, including monthly renewals, the unused part of a billing period and unused credits. Cancelling stops future renewals but doesn't refund the billing period you've already paid for: your subscription stays active until that period ends. When you switch between monthly and yearly billing, the unused part of your current period is credited toward the new billing interval rather than paid back.

## Credits used by workflows [#credits]

Credits used by a workflow aren't returned, whether a task fails, you cancel the workflow or the result isn't what you wanted. Tasks run on your computer with your own agents, so a task failing there isn't in itself a problem on CodeDAG's side.

If a problem on CodeDAG's side kept a workflow from running properly, or credits were deducted incorrectly, write to us. If we confirm the problem, we restore the affected credits to your account. Restoring credits corrects your balance; it isn't a refund of money.

## How to ask [#request]

Write to <Email user="hi" /> from the email address of your CodeDAG account, or include that address. Tell us which payment or workflow your request is about. A Stripe receipt or invoice, if you have one, helps us find the payment.

You don't need to give a reason for the 14-day refund. If you're willing, telling us what went wrong or what didn't work for you helps us improve CodeDAG. It doesn't affect your refund either way.

Refunds go to the original payment method through Stripe. How long the money takes to reach you depends on Stripe and your bank.

## Changes to this policy [#changes]

We change this policy in the same way as the Terms, as described in [Changes to these Terms](/terms#changes). A refund request is handled under the version that was in effect when you were charged.
