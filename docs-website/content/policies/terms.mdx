## Credits [#credits]

Submitting a workflow and waiting in the queue cost nothing, and a workflow cancelled before it starts costs nothing. Once a workflow has started, its credits aren't returned automatically, whatever happens to its tasks. The exceptions are set out in the [Refund Policy](/refund).

Credits have no cash value, can't be exchanged for money and can't be transferred between accounts.
