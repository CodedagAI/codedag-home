This Privacy Policy explains how Zillion AI LLC ("Zillion AI", "we", "us") collects, uses and shares personal data when you use CodeDAG: the command-line tool (the "CLI"), the web console at app.codedag.com and the website at codedag.com (together, the "Service"). Zillion AI is the controller of this data. This policy is part of our [Terms of Service](/terms).

CodeDAG runs on your computer. Your code, run logs and agent sessions stay there. We collect only what we need to run your account, count credits and handle payments.

## Information we collect [#what-we-collect]

### Information you provide [#you-provide]

- **Account information.** You sign in with your Google account, and we receive your name, email address, profile picture and Google account ID. While CodeDAG is invite-only, an account can be created only for an email address on our invite list, which we build from the waitlist and from individual invitations.
- **Payment information.** On the payment page, we fill in your billing region, such as your country and, where tax rules require it, a postal code, from your earlier choice or from the approximate location of your IP address, and you can change it. If paid plans are available there, we send your billing region to Stripe to calculate tax, even if you don't go on to subscribe. You enter your card details in the payment form of our payment processor, Stripe, and we never receive your full card number. When you subscribe, we record when you agreed to the renewal terms, the wording you were shown and the price.
- **Waitlist.** If you join the waitlist, our form provider collects your email address, your answers to a few optional questions and the page that brought you to the form.
- **Communications.** When you write to us, we keep your message, your email address and any attachments. If you open an issue in our public GitHub repository, it's visible to everyone and handled under GitHub's terms.

### Information collected when you use the Service [#usage]

- **Workflow files.** To count and deduct credits, the CLI sends us the workflow file you run, including its prompts and any code written in it, along with the tasks to run and technical details such as the CLI version and operating system. We use the file only to count credits and compute a checksum, then discard its contents. They aren't stored in our database or our logs. [Where your data goes](/docs/account/login-and-account#data-boundary) explains exactly what the CLI sends and when.
- **Usage and credit records.** For each workflow that starts, we keep a record such as the time, the tasks, the credits deducted and the checksum of the workflow file, which can't be used to recover the file. We also keep your credit balance and any changes we make to it, such as restoring credits under the [Refund Policy](/refund#credits).
- **Device and log information.** Our servers and service providers record technical information about requests to the Service, such as your IP address, your browser or CLI version, your operating system, the time of the request and any errors.
- **Website analytics.** codedag.com uses a cookieless analytics service to count visits in aggregate. It records information such as the pages you view, the referring page, your browser and device type, your approximate location and the links you click, but it can't recognize you across days or across other websites.

The code in your repository, your run logs and your agents' sessions aren't sent to us. Your coding agents, such as Claude Code and Codex, send code and context to their model providers directly from your computer, under those providers' terms, and that traffic doesn't pass through us. The CLI and the web console don't include product usage analytics.

### Information from third parties [#third-parties]

Stripe sends us records of your subscription and payments, such as your plan, billing dates and amounts, your billing address and the country where your card was issued. Stripe's payment software on our checkout page may also collect information about your device and how you use the page, to prevent fraud. Stripe handles that information under its [Privacy Policy](https://stripe.com/privacy).

## How we use information [#use]

We use personal data to:

- provide the Service, including sign-in, your account, credits and subscriptions, and, while CodeDAG is invite-only, decide who can create an account and which free credits it receives;
- process payments, calculate taxes, confirm that paid plans are available in your region, and prevent fraud and duplicate charges;
- send you messages about your account, credits and subscription, and answer your requests;
- invite waitlist members to the beta, announce the launch, and understand how people want to use CodeDAG;
- keep the Service secure, and investigate errors and abuse;
- understand, in aggregate, how the website is used, so that we can improve it;
- meet legal obligations, such as tax, accounting and consumer protection.

We don't sell personal data or share it for advertising, and we don't use your workflow files, prompts or code to train AI models.

## Legal bases [#legal-bases]

We process personal data on these legal bases:

- **Contract:** to provide the Service you signed up for, including your account, credits and payments.
- **Legitimate interests:** to secure and debug the Service, prevent fraud, limit sign-up during the beta and improve the website.
- **Consent:** to email you about the beta and the launch after you join the waitlist. You can withdraw your consent at any time.
- **Legal obligation:** to keep the records that tax, accounting and consumer protection laws require.

## How we share information [#sharing]

We share personal data only in these cases:

- **Service providers.** We use companies that help us run the Service, in areas such as hosting and databases, sign-in, payment processing and tax calculation, error tracking, logging and uptime monitoring, website analytics, forms, email, and code hosting and automation. Each receives only the data it needs for its task. Some, such as our payment processor, also handle data under their own privacy policies.
- **Legal requirements and safety.** We may disclose personal data when the law requires it, or when it's needed to protect the rights and safety of our users or the Service.
- **Business transfers.** If Zillion AI is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, personal data may be transferred as part of that transaction, and this policy will continue to apply to it.

## Cookies and similar technologies [#cookies]

codedag.com doesn't use cookies. It remembers display preferences, such as the light or dark theme, in your browser's local storage, which stays on your device. app.codedag.com uses only the cookies it needs to work, such as those that keep you signed in and remember the language you choose. We don't use advertising cookies. The payment form, the billing portal and the waitlist form are run by our payment and form providers, which may use their own cookies.

We don't track you across other websites, so we don't respond differently to Do Not Track signals. Apart from the analytics and the providers described in this policy, we don't allow third parties to collect information about your activity on our sites.

## International transfers [#transfers]

Zillion AI is based in the United States, and our service providers process data in the United States and in other countries where they operate. Our team may also access data from other countries. Where the law requires safeguards for these transfers, we rely on our service providers' contractual commitments to protect the data to a comparable standard, for example the standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission and their UK equivalents for data from the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom. To request a copy, contact us.

## How long we keep information [#retention]

We keep personal data only as long as we need it for the purposes described in this policy, and then delete or anonymize it:

- **Account, credit, subscription and billing records:** while your account exists. After your account is deleted, we keep subscription and billing records only as long as tax, accounting or other legal obligations require.
- **Workflow file contents:** not kept. They're discarded as soon as credits are counted.
- **Logs and error reports:** for a short period, as needed to run, debug and secure the Service. We may keep specific records longer to investigate a security incident or abuse, or to meet a legal obligation.
- **Waitlist and invite list entries:** until we no longer need them for invitations and the launch, or until you ask us to remove them.
- **Emails:** as long as needed to handle your request and keep a record of it.

## Your rights [#rights]

Depending on where you live, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, California or Japan, you may have the right to:

- **Access** the personal data we hold about you, and get a copy in a portable format;
- **Correct** data that's inaccurate;
- **Delete** your account and personal data, or be removed from the waitlist;
- **Object to or restrict** certain processing;
- **Withdraw** a consent you've given.

To make a request, write to <Email user="privacy" /> from the email address of your CodeDAG account, so that we can confirm the account is yours. If you want your account deleted and have an active subscription, cancel the subscription first so that it doesn't renew. We won't treat you differently for exercising these rights. If you have a concern or complaint about how we handle your data, write to the same address. We'll respond to requests and complaints within 20 working days. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can also complain to the data protection authority where you live.

## Security [#security]

We keep only the data described in this policy, encrypt connections to our servers with HTTPS, and limit who can access personal data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we'll notify you as the law requires.

## Children [#children]

The Service is meant for people aged 18 or older (see the [Terms of Service](/terms#accounts)). We don't knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has given us personal data, write to us and we'll delete it.
